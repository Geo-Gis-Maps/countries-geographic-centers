About This Project

Countries Geographic Centers is an open geographic reference — a clean, fast, multilingual atlas of the exact mathematical center of every sovereign country on Earth.

What We Built

🌍

196 Countries

Every UN-recognized sovereign state plus key territories, each with a precisely calculated center point.

📐

WGS84 Precision

Area-weighted centroid calculated on the WGS84 ellipsoid — the same standard used by GPS systems worldwide.

🗺️

Interactive Map

All 196 centers plotted on a live Leaflet map. Click any pin to open that country's detail page.

🌐

5 Languages

English, Turkish, German, Spanish and Portuguese — switch instantly with no page reload.

⚡

Fast & Lightweight

No heavy frameworks. Pure HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Loads in under a second.

🌙

Dark / Light Mode

Comfortable to use at any time of day. Your preference is saved locally.

Data & Accuracy

All coordinate data is sourced from high-resolution GeoJSON boundaries and calculated using the area-weighted centroid method. Accuracy is typically ±3–7 meters. See the Methodology page for full details.

Open & Transparent

This project is built on open data and open-source tools: OpenStreetMap tiles, Leaflet.js, Natural Earth boundaries, and Inter typeface by Rasmus Andersson.

🔗

Based on data from Middle of Countries

Geographic center calculations and boundary data are derived from middleofcountries.github.io. We are grateful for their meticulous work in computing these coordinates.
